### 3. Reducing T gates in product formulas with agent loops

**来源**: arXiv quant-ph  
**时间**: 4小时前  
**链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33509

### 4. Reproducible distributions of energy-relaxation times in superconducting qubits from broadband SWAP spectroscopy

### 5. Direct-Coupled Architectures for Magic-State Factories
