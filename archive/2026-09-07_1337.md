### 2. A Sim-to-Real Study of Surface-Code Decoder Benchmarking

**来源**: arXiv quant-ph  
**时间**: 1小时前  
**链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04557

### 4. Compiling the 2D Fermi-Hubbard ground-state energy estimation algorithm for active volume quantum architectures
